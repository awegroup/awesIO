### Added

- Optional `winches` columns for the drivetrain: `inertia` (kg*m^2), the moment of inertia of motor, gearbox and drum as seen from the motor shaft, and `friction_coulomb` (N) and `friction_viscous` (N*s/m), the drivetrain friction as a force at the tether. `N*s/m` joins the units on the conventions page.
